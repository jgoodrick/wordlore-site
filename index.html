Wordlore

A vocabulary journal for people living in a second language. Keep the words you meet, see what they mean, and bring them back before you forget them.

Keep the words you meet

Type a word and Wordlore looks it up as you go. Add it, and it is in your collection with its meaning.

Bring them back

Reps schedules each word for review, so you see it again just before you would have forgotten it.

Yours, on your devices

For iPhone, iPad and Mac. There is no account. Your collection syncs through your own iCloud, and the developer never sees it. Read the privacy policy.
