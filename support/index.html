Support

Write to support@josephgoodrick.com. One person makes Wordlore and reads every message.

When something goes wrong

It helps to say which device you are using, the app version, the word you were adding or reviewing, and what you expected to happen. The version is in Settings, under About. To open Settings, tap the person button at the top of the screen.

How do I add a word?

Tap the + button at the top of the screen, choose the language, and type the word. Wordlore looks it up as you type. Tap Add, and the word is in your collection.

The app doesn't know my word

Wordlore looks words up in dictionaries you download, one per language. Open Settings, then Dictionaries, and download the dictionary for the language you are learning. Dictionaries work offline once downloaded. You can always add a word the dictionary doesn't know and write its meaning yourself.

My words aren't on my other device

Wordlore syncs through your own iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud is turned on for Wordlore in the Settings app, and that both are online. Sync can take a few minutes the first time.

Where are scanning, speaking and handwriting?

They are optional extras, off until you switch them on. Open Settings, then Features. Switching one off hides it and keeps everything you saved.

Why is Reps empty?

A word you add today comes up for review from tomorrow. Reps shows the words that are due.

How do I delete my data?

See the privacy policy. Wordlore has no account and the developer holds none of your data, so there is nothing to ask the developer to delete.
